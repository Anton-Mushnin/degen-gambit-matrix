# GuessNextNumber - Commands

## guess [1-6]
Play the game by guessing a number from 1 to 6.
Uses commit-reveal with auto-accept.

## deposit [amount]
Fund the bank. 1% fee applied.
Player receives share: (amount - fee) / (bank + amount)

## withdraw [amount]
Withdraw ETH from bank based on player's share.
Free, no fees.
