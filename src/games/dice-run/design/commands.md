# Dice Run Commands

## play <number>
Bet on a number (1-6). If your number matches the random roll, you win. Consecutive wins on same or sequential numbers build a streak. Streak of 3+ wins pays bonus from the bank.

## fund <amount>
Invest any amount (in WEI) into the bank. A small fee is charged. You receive ownership shares and can withdraw your portion later.

## withdraw <amount>
Withdraw up to your share of the bank (in WEI) based on your ownership percentage.
